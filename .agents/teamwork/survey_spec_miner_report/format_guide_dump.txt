SPECIFICATIONS FOR PROJECT REPORT 
1. The PROJECT REPORT shall be typed or printed on A4 size executive bond paper. 
No hand written PROJECT REPORT will be accepted. 
2. The PROJECT REPORT shall be typed on both side with 1½ spacing with a margin of 3.5 cms on the left 
2.5cms on the top and 1.25 cms on the right and bottom. (as per annexure I) 
3. In a PROJECT REPORT, the Cover page is given (as per annexure II) 
4. the title page should be given first, then the certificate by the candidate and the mentors 
followed by an abstract of the PROJECT REPORT not exceeding 300 words. This should be 
followed by acknowledgement and a table of contents. (as per annexure III) 
5. The references should be given at the end of the PROJECT REPORT  preferably in 
alphabetic order of the authors’ names. 
6. In the body of the text, a reference should be indicated by a number in parenthesis such as 
[5].These references should be listed giving (i) the author’s name and his initials (ii) the title 
of the paper and the name of the journal (iii) the name of the book and the publisher (iv) the 
number of volume, page numbers and the year of public ation. Standard abbreviations may be 
used in the names of the journals. 
7. The diagrams should be all in ink or should be printed on a light background. Tabular matter 
should be clearly arranged. Decimal points may be indicated by a full-stop. 
8. The PROJECT REPORT shall be spiral bound at the time of initial submission. 
9. After the viva -voce examination the PROJECT REPORT shall be submitted in hard bound. 
The PROJECT REPORT shall be bound in card sheet paper of maroon colour for final PG and 
dark gray for final undergraduate semester.  The name of the candidate, the degree and the year 
shall be printed in bold on the cover in gold (as per annexure II), For PG second and UG fourth 
semester, the project report will be spiral binded
